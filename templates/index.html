Overview

What is Upward?

Upward is an AI-powered career and financial resilience platform that helps you make smarter long-term decisions about your career, skills, income, and future opportunities. Unlike generic tools, Upward gives you personalized, data-driven guidance based on your unique profile — not generic advice.

Audience

Who are we targeting?

  • High school and university students planning their future
  • Early-career professionals looking to grow faster
  • Job seekers who want a competitive edge
  • Freelancers and career changers ready to level up
  • Anyone asking — what should I learn next?
Features

What you will get

  • AI Career Advisor — personalized roadmaps and skill recommendations
  • Future Risk Score — understand your career vulnerabilities
  • Financial Resilience Score — measure your ability to withstand setbacks
  • Skill Tree — visual map of what to learn next
  • What-If Simulator — test scenarios before committing
  • Weekly Growth Plans — actionable steps tailored to you
Opportunities

Opportunity Finder

Upward ranks career opportunities based on income potential, learning difficulty, time investment, and market demand. Whether it is AI Engineering, Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, or Software Development — you will know exactly where to invest your time for the highest return.

Simulation

What-If Simulator

Test future scenarios before making life-changing decisions. What if I learn Machine Learning? What if I move to Germany? What if I change careers entirely? Upward estimates your possible future outcomes and career trajectories based on real market data.

Progress

Growth Tracking

Upward tracks your skills learned, projects completed, certifications earned, and career milestones. Your AI advisor continuously updates its recommendations as you grow — so your roadmap always reflects where you actually are, not where you started.

Vision

Why it matters

Most people make career decisions based on guesswork. Upward uses data, AI, and market trends to help you increase your earning potential, reduce career risk, and future-proof your skills — becoming your personalized AI mentor for career growth and financial resilience.
